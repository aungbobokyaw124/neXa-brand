# neXa Logo Decisions

## Decision 001

The brand definition is locked as:

> neXa — Next-Generation Technology Ecosystem

Positioning:

> AI-powered technology for the next generation.

Tagline:

> Build Beyond.

## Decision 002

The initial visual direction uses a geometric nexus symbol to communicate connection, technology, and an ecosystem.

## Decision 003

The initial SVG is a **concept foundation**, not yet the legally registered or permanently final trademark artwork.

The logo must pass a human design review and trademark conflict research before being treated as the final production mark.
