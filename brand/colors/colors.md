# neXa Color System

## Core Palette

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Primary Navy | `#0B1F3A` |
| Primary Emerald | `#10B981` |
| White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Deep Navy | `#071426` |

## Usage

- Navy is the primary technology background.
- Emerald is the main brand accent.
- White is used for high-contrast wordmarks and typography.
- Deep Navy is an optional supporting dark surface.

## Rule

Do not introduce new brand colors into the core identity without documenting and approving them through the brand repository.
