# neXa Brand Guidelines — Foundation

## Brand

**neXa**  
**Next-Generation Technology Ecosystem**

**AI-powered technology for the next generation.**

**Build Beyond.**

## Logo Family

- `brand/logo/nexa-logo.svg` — primary presentation logo
- `brand/logo/nexa-icon.svg` — symbol/app icon direction
- `brand/logo/nexa-monochrome.svg` — one-color usage

## Clear Space

Maintain generous empty space around the mark. Do not place competing graphics or text inside the immediate logo area.

## Minimum Size

The symbol must remain recognizable at small digital sizes. Validate final minimum sizes after the logo geometry is approved.

## Do

- Use official repository assets.
- Preserve proportions.
- Keep sufficient contrast.
- Use the approved color palette.
- Use monochrome when color reproduction is unavailable.

## Don't

- Stretch or compress the logo.
- Rotate the logo.
- Change the symbol geometry.
- Add unapproved gradients or effects.
- Recreate the logo manually.
- Combine neXa with third-party logos into one mark.

## Status

This is the foundation guideline. Final production specifications will be added after the logo geometry is formally approved.
