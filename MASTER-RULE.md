# neXa MASTER BRAND RULE

**Brand Name:** neXa  
**Meaning:** Next-Generation Technology Ecosystem  
**Positioning:** AI-powered technology for the next generation.  
**Short Tagline:** Build Beyond.  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Foundation Date:** 2026-08-31

## 1. Purpose
This repository is the canonical source of truth for the neXa brand identity, logo assets, visual standards, and brand history.

## 2. Core Identity
neXa is a long-term technology ecosystem spanning AI, web, cloud, automation, APIs, digital services, education, software, and infrastructure.

## 3. Logo Principles
- The official master logo is vector-first.
- SVG is the source format.
- The symbol must remain recognizable at small sizes.
- The logo must work in full-color, dark, light, and monochrome contexts.
- Do not stretch, rotate, distort, redraw, or add unapproved effects.

## 4. Originality
The neXa identity must be original and must not intentionally copy another company's mark. AI tools may assist with exploration, but the controlled vector master is the official design source.

## 5. Visual System
Primary background: `#0B1F3A`  
Primary accent: `#10B981`  
Primary light: `#FFFFFF`  
Primary dark: `#071426`

Design language: minimal, geometric, technical, modern, premium, precise.

## 6. Naming
Official spelling: **neXa**. Do not casually substitute NEXA, Nexa, nexa, or NeXa in brand-facing materials.

## 7. Third-Party Logos
Platform logos remain separate assets and remain subject to their respective owners' trademark and brand-use rules. They must never be altered to imply ownership by neXa.

## 8. Version Control
Every meaningful brand change must be recorded in Git history and documented in `CHANGELOG.md`.

Recommended commit style:
- `feat: establish neXa brand foundation`
- `refactor: refine neXa logo geometry`
- `docs: update brand guidelines`

## 9. Legal Status
This repository documents creation and development of the neXa brand. A Git repository or original artwork does not by itself constitute trademark registration. No registration claim may be made until the appropriate trademark process is completed.

## 10. Source of Truth
Projects using neXa must source official logo assets from this repository rather than manually recreating the logo.

## 11. Permanent Principle
Future versions may extend this rule, but the following principles remain fundamental:

**Originality — Consistency — Vector Master — Version Control — Legal Responsibility**
